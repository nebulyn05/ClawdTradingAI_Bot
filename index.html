The most common questions, answered honestly. Still unsure? Ask us directly in the Telegram community.

It is a Telegram bot with an AI brain and a fast execution backbone. You start the bot, create a wallet inside it, and deposit. From there the AI watches the four supported chains for launches, narrative flow, smart money moves, KOL posts, and cross-chain spreads, then routes orders through a pre-trained execution stack: rug screening, priority bundles, MEV-safe routing, slippage optimization. You do not install anything. You do not manage API keys.
The AI does not race the market in microseconds. That is the job of the infrastructure. The AI reads signals (price structure, wallet activity, social mentions, contract metadata) and picks which of five built-in specialists should handle the order: Sniper for launches, Scout for smart money and KOL flow, Guard for rug screening, Arbiter for cross-chain spreads, and Router for the best fill. Execution itself runs on dedicated RPC nodes, mempool scanning, and MEV-protected bundles.
No. Your private key never leaves your device unencrypted. The bot generates a fresh wallet, encrypts the key with AES-256-GCM against a passphrase only you know, and stores the ciphertext. Without that passphrase, no one (including us) can decrypt or move your funds. You can export the raw key anytime with /export.
Instant. No lockup, no staking, no vesting. Send /withdraw
and the funds move on-chain the next block. Solana under 2 seconds, EVM chains 12 to 15 seconds.
Worst case breach, attackers see ciphertext. Without your passphrase it is unusable. You can always export the raw key and move funds manually. 30-day rolling uptime is 99.39%.
2% of the profit on closed wins. Nothing otherwise. No subscription, no seat cost, no minimum volume. The bot is built to close winners fast and cut losers faster, so if it does not make you money, it does not make us money.
Those bots are built around raw speed for new launches. That only matters in one market state. Clawd Agents adds a layer on top: the AI picks the right specialist (Sniper, Scout, Guard, Arbiter, Router) for whatever the market is doing, so the product is not idle between launches. Fee structure also differs. Maestro and peers charge monthly plus per trade regardless of outcome. Clawd Agents charges 2% only on profitable trades.
Solana, Ethereum, BSC, Base, Monad, and Robinhood. Dozens of DEXes routed automatically, including Raydium, Jupiter, Pump.fun, Uniswap, PancakeSwap, Fourmeme, and more. The Router picks the best fill per order across all of them.
