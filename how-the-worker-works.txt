HOW THE TRADING WORKER WORKS
=============================

OVERVIEW
--------
The worker is the automated engine that finds trading opportunities, checks
them for safety, executes trades, and manages open positions until they're
closed. It runs continuously in the background, with every chain currently
defaulting to testnet (no real funds at risk until deliberately switched to
mainnet).

STEP 1: FINDING OPPORTUNITIES
------------------------------
Three independent signal sources watch the market simultaneously:

- Sniper - watches each blockchain in real time for brand-new token
  launches / trading pairs the moment they appear.
- Scout (wallet tracking) - watches known "smart money" wallets; if one of
  them buys a token, that's a signal.
- Scout (social tracking) - monitors a configured list of Twitter/X
  accounts (KOLs) for token calls, using AI to judge whether a tweet is
  actually calling a token rather than just mentioning one.
- Arbiter - separately scans for the same asset trading at different
  prices across chains. This one is detection-only right now - it logs
  opportunities but doesn't automatically execute a buy-bridge-sell
  sequence yet.

STEP 2: SAFETY SCREENING ("GUARD")
------------------------------------
Every candidate token goes through two layers before any money is risked:

Mechanical checks (rules-based, no AI): Is there enough liquidity? Can the
token creator mint unlimited new supply or freeze holders' tokens? Is it a
honeypot (can be bought but not sold)? Is ownership concentrated in a few
wallets? Is the sell tax unreasonably high? Each issue subtracts points
from a 100-point score; it needs to score 60+ to pass.

AI review (final gate, optional): if the mechanical checks pass, AI is
shown the results and asked to catch anything they'd miss - like a token
name impersonating a known project. It defaults to approving unless it
finds something concrete, and if the AI call fails, it defaults to
rejecting rather than silently letting the trade through.

STEP 3: RISK CHECKS BEFORE BUYING
------------------------------------
Even after Guard approves a token, three more checks run:

1. Circuit breaker - if a chain's realized losses have exceeded a set
   threshold recently, new trades on that chain pause automatically
   (existing positions are still managed normally). This never
   auto-resumes - a human has to turn it back on.
2. Exposure cap - rejects a trade if it would push a user's total exposure
   on that chain above a set percentage of their overall portfolio value.
3. Position sizing - calculates how much to bet based on that signal
   source's historical win rate (once enough trade history exists); starts
   conservative/flat-sized until there's real data to learn from.

If all checks pass, the trade executes and the position is recorded with
an automatic take-profit and stop-loss target.

STEP 4: MANAGING THE POSITION (SELLING)
------------------------------------------
Every open position is re-checked on a short interval:

1. Safety re-check first - if a token that was safe at purchase now fails
   the safety screen (e.g. liquidity was pulled, it looks like a rug), the
   position is sold immediately regardless of price.
2. Price target check - otherwise, if the price has hit the take-profit or
   stop-loss level, it sells.

Separately, on a much slower interval, AI can re-evaluate and adjust the
take-profit/stop-loss percentages on positions that are still open -
widening targets on strong momentum, tightening the stop-loss if the
token's safety score has degraded. If this fails or is turned off, the
position simply keeps its existing targets - nothing breaks.

THE APIS IT USES, AND WHAT EACH ONE IS FOR
---------------------------------------------

Blockchain access:
- Helius - Solana network access: reads balances, submits transactions,
  and feeds real-time detection of new token launches.
- Alchemy / QuickNode - the equivalent for the Ethereum/BSC/Base networks.

Executing trades:
- Jupiter - finds the best swap route and executes trades on Solana.
- Jito (optional) - bundles a Solana trade with a small tip so it can't be
  front-run by bots watching the network.
- 1inch - finds the best swap route and executes trades on
  Ethereum/BSC/Base.
- Flashbots (optional, Ethereum only) - submits trades privately so they
  can't be front-run.

Prices:
- Birdeye - primary price source for Solana tokens.
- DexScreener - backup price source for any chain, picks the most liquid
  trading pool for a token.
- Pyth - price feed used specifically for the value of each chain's native
  currency (SOL, ETH, BNB).

Safety screening:
- GoPlus - free token-security data: mint/freeze authority, honeypot
  detection, holder concentration, sell tax, and more, for both Solana and
  EVM-based tokens.

Cross-chain:
- LI.FI - gets real bridge quotes across chains, used to detect and price
  cross-chain arbitrage opportunities.

Social signal:
- X (Twitter) API - pulls tweets from the configured list of accounts
  being tracked for token calls.

AI:
- Anthropic (Claude) - used for three specific jobs: judging whether a
  tweet is a genuine token call, a final safety sanity-check after the
  mechanical rules pass, and periodically tuning take-profit/stop-loss
  targets on open positions. AI never decides when to buy or sell on its
  own - those triggers are deterministic (a score threshold, a price
  level, a loss threshold); AI only shapes some of the inputs to those
  rules.

Note: two data sources exist in the system's toolkit but aren't actively
used yet - a Chainlink price feed integration (built but not currently
called anywhere) and Etherscan (used only for a read-only transaction
history view in the admin dashboard, not for any trading decision).
