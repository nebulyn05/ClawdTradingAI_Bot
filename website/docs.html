Full walkthrough

Open the chat

Find @ClawdTradingAI_Bot in Telegram and tap /start. A non-custodial wallet is generated right there in chat — one keypair covers all six chains. No install, no signup, no email.

Fund the wallet

Pick the chain you want to start on — Solana, Ethereum, BNB Chain, Base, Monad, or Robinhood Chain — and send any amount to that deposit address. The agent trades there by default; if volume on that chain dries up, it automatically routes to a livelier one (deposit on BNB, end up sniping on Solana when the action moves).

Common questions

Things people ask before they tap.

Do I need an API key or to install anything?

No. Open Telegram, find @ClawdTradingAI_Bot, tap /start. The bot generates your wallet inside the chat — that's the entire setup.

Is my private key safe?

The wallet is non-custodial. Your private key is encrypted at rest and bound to your Telegram account; we never see plaintext keys. You can export the raw key any time from /wallet → Export.

How does the bot avoid rug pulls?

Every order passes a 4-layer screen: locked liquidity, deployer reputation, live honeypot test, and contract scan. If any check fails, the trade never fires. See Rug Protection for the full breakdown.

What does it cost?

2% on profitable exits only. Losing trades pay nothing. There's no subscription, no monthly fee, no hidden gas markup.

Which chains are supported?

Solana, Ethereum, BNB Chain, Base, Monad, and Robinhood Chain. Deposit on the chain you want to start trading on — the agent runs there by default, then auto-routes to whichever chain has the strongest volume when the action moves.
