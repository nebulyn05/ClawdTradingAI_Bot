How routing works

The Router compares all 33 venues per order.

For every entry, the Router agent computes the effective fill price across every venue available on the chain — including price impact, gas, and fee tier — and picks the best one. The fill never relies on a single AMM's assumptions.

MEV protection sits on top: every transaction goes through a private relay so sandwich attacks and front-runs can't see your order before it lands.

And when the chain you started on goes quiet, the agent doesn't sit idle. It tracks volume across all six chains and migrates to whichever one has the freshest action — deposit on BNB, end up sniping on Solana when the action lands there.
