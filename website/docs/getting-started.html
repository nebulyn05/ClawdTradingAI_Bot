Getting Started

Three taps and the AI is trading memecoins on your behalf. No install, no accounts, no charts to read — everything happens inside Telegram.

What you need

  • A Telegram account (mobile or desktop)
  • A native token to deposit (SOL, ETH, BNB, or anything we support)
  • That's it. No downloads, extensions, or signups.
STEP 01

Open the bot

Send /start to @ClawdTradingAI_Bot on Telegram. A menu pops up with everything you need.

No app install. No browser extension. No API keys. Works the same on phone, desktop, and web Telegram.

STEP 02

Create a wallet, deposit

Tap Wallet → Generate. The bot returns one address that works for Solana, Ethereum, BSC, Base, Monad, and Robinhood. Send any native coin to start.

Your private key is yours — exportable anytime. Deposits land within seconds and the bot confirms the amount in your chat with the live USD value.

STEP 03

Let the AI trade

Once a deposit lands, the AI watches the market 24/7 — meme launches, smart-money moves, cross-chain spreads — and trades automatically on your behalf.

Every entry, exit, and PnL update arrives in your chat as it happens. Pause or take manual control any time from the menu.
