Auto Take-Profit

Exits, calibrated live.

Manual stop-loss and take-profit targets only work if you can read the market. Most can't — memecoin volatility flips between regimes too fast. Clawd Agents reads the regime live, sets the targets, and adjusts them while the position is open.

How auto-tuning works

On every entry, the agent samples the recent price tape — last 60 seconds, 5 minutes, 30 minutes — and classifies the trade into a volatility regime: low-vol grind, trending pump, parabolic blow-off, or rejection chop.

Each regime gets a different target template:

  • • Low-vol grind — tight TP (≈ 8 %), tight SL (≈ -5 %), no trailing.
  • • Trending pump — medium TP (≈ 25 %), wider SL (≈ -10 %), trailing on.
  • • Parabolic blow-off — staged exits (1/3 at +50 %, 1/3 at +120 %, last 1/3 trailing), aggressive SL.
  • • Rejection chop — fast exit on first sign of reversal, no trailing.
Four ways a position closes

Every position has four ways out.

Take-profit reached

Auto target is recalculated live based on the current volatility regime. When the position hits it, Clawd exits immediately into the best venue. Profitable trade closed.

Stop-loss tripped

If the position drops below the dynamic stop level, the agent exits to preserve the remaining capital. Losing trade closed; no fee charged.

Trailing stop triggered

Once profit > 2× the original stop distance, the stop trails the high water mark. Locks gains during parabolic moves without capping the upside.

Guard detects rug pattern mid-trade

If the rugscreen flags suspicious post-entry behavior (LP unlocked mid-trade, deployer dumps, contract proxy change), Clawd exits instantly — even if no price target was hit.

Want to fix the targets yourself?

Open /settings → Stop-loss and pick a fixed percentage (-10 / -15 / -20). Take-profit can also be locked to a fraction. Manual overrides apply to all subsequent trades; existing open positions keep their auto targets.
