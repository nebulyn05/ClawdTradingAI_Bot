Referrals

Refer friends. Earn on every win.

When someone joins via your link and closes a profitable trade, you get paid. No caps, no expiry, no claims to file — payouts route directly to your set wallet.

How it works

Three taps to a working referral.

01

Open /referral

Inside Telegram, type /referral or tap 🎁 Rewards from the main menu. Your referral link + code is generated on the spot. Nothing to fill out.

02

Share the link or code

Paste your link in Telegram, X, Discord, Reddit — anywhere. New users who tap /start with your code get attributed automatically. One-shot, can't be replayed.

03

Earn forever, on every win

Whenever a referred user closes a profitable trade, your cut routes to the wallet you set under /referral → Settings. Same chain, same token they traded in. No claims, no waiting periods, no thresholds.

What makes it different

  • Lifetime tracking — once attributed, always attributed
  • Multi-tier (3 levels deep, declining percentages)
  • Paid on every chain the referee trades on — no conversions
  • Auto-routes to your set rewards wallet, no manual claims
  • Fully transparent — see total earned + per-referee breakdown in /referral
Worked example

You refer 5 traders. They trade actively for a month.

  • 5 traders × $10K monthly profit each = $50K combined profit
  • Platform fee at 2% = $1,000 in fees collected
  • Your direct cut at 30% = $300 routed to your wallet
  • Your tier-2 cut from their referrals (if any) compounds on top — paid the same way
